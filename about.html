About this site

Written by Brahim Ben Sliman · Updated October 2026
The short version: I got tired of gadget reviews that read like adverts, so I started writing the opposite — buying guides that tell you what to check, what to skip, and what the honest price band looks like before you spend anything.

How the guides are made

  • Topics come from what UK homes are actually searching and buying right now — seasonal demand first (heating, damp, drying clothes, kitchen, home office).
  • Every guide is written for one job: helping you decide. What to spend, what features matter, what to avoid, and when the honest answer is "buy nothing".
  • No paid placements. No brand gets a better write-up for anything. Where a guide has a "catch" section, read it — that's the part most review sites hide.
  • Guides are refreshed when the products or the advice change, not just restamped with a new date.

How the site earns money

Plainly: affiliate links. When a link on this site is an Amazon link, buying something through it can earn a small commission — it costs you nothing extra, and it never changes what gets recommended. Every page with affiliate links says so near the top. The longer-term plan is display advertising once there's enough traffic to make it worth anyone's time; if that happens, it will be announced on these pages with a clear ad-labelling policy.
