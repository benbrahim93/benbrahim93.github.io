Privacy policy

Updated 14 October 2026 · Plain English, on purpose
Short version: this site collects no personal data. There are no forms, no logins, no analytics and no trackers — it's a handful of static pages with products I recommend. The only cookies you'll encounter are set by third parties when you click an affiliate link. That's explained below.

What I collect

Nothing. This site is static — it runs off plain HTML files on GitHub Pages. No contact forms, no comment systems, no analytics scripts, no user accounts. I don't know who you are, where you are, or what you looked at.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links, mainly to Amazon. If you click one and buy something, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. The links are marked with ↗ and every page says so near the top.

Those links are operated by third parties (Amazon Associates and, in the links section, referral programmes). If you click one, that company may set its own cookies and process data under its own privacy policy, not mine:

  • Amazon UK: Amazon's privacy notice
  • Coinbase and Binance: both publish their own privacy policies on their sites

Advertising

There is currently no advertising on this site. If that changes, this section will be updated — and if a network like Google AdSense is used, it may set advertising cookies, in which case a consent notice will be shown before any of that happens, as UK/EU rules require.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

Because nothing is collected, there's nothing to access, correct, export or delete. If you want to check anything anyway, or you'd like a request handled, write to me: contact via tiktok

You also have the right to complain to the UK regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk

Changes

If the site ever starts collecting data, running analytics or carrying advertising, this page changes first — with the date updated at the top and the section that changed spelled out.
